How it works

Up and running in minutes.

1

Add SkipKey to your keyboards

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → SkipKey. Enable Allow Full Access.

2

Switch to SkipKey in any app

Tap the globe icon on your keyboard to switch. SkipKey appears alongside your regular keyboard.

3

Tap Start Recording and speak

SkipKey opens briefly to start recording. Return to your app and speak naturally in any language.

4

Transcript inserts automatically

Tap Stop Recording in the keyboard. Your words are transcribed on-device and placed into your text field.
