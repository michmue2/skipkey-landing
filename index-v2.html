02 — How it works

Up and running
in minutes.

01

Add SkipKey to your keyboards

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → SkipKey. Enable Allow Full Access.

02

Switch to SkipKey in any app

Tap the globe icon on your keyboard to switch. SkipKey appears alongside your regular keyboard.

03

Tap Start Recording and speak

SkipKey opens briefly to start recording. Return to your app and speak naturally in any language.

04

Transcript inserts automatically

Tap Stop Recording. Your words are transcribed on-device and placed into your text field.

03 — AI Post-Processing

Your workflow,
your rules.

Optionally connect your preferred LLM provider and define custom modes that transform every transcript the moment you stop recording. Name the mode, write a one-line instruction — SkipKey applies it hands-free.

German for Auntie
You said

"Hey auntie, just wanted to say I'll be visiting in July — super excited to see you and the whole family!"

↓
SkipKey inserted

"Hallo Tante, ich wollte nur kurz sagen, dass ich im Juli vorbeikomme — ich freue mich so sehr, dich und die ganze Familie zu sehen!"

Work — Semi-Formal
You said

"yeah so we need to push the deadline back like a week or two, the API stuff just isn't done yet"

↓
SkipKey inserted

"We need to extend the deadline by one to two weeks, as the API integration is not yet complete."

Set up any mode in the app — give it a name, write a prompt, and the button appears in the keyboard. Switch modes with a single tap between recordings.
