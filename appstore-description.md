# SkipKey — App Store Description

---

SkipKey is private dictation for your iPhone. Speak into any app — your voice is transcribed on-device and inserted automatically.

No cloud. No account. No subscription.

**Works everywhere.**
SkipKey is a real iOS keyboard. Switch to it in Messages, Notes, Mail — anywhere you type. Tap Start Recording, speak, tap Stop. Done.

**25 languages. Zero setup.**
Speak naturally in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, and 20 more. SkipKey detects your language automatically.

**Your own post-processing flows. Your own AI.**
Optionally connect your preferred LLM provider and define custom modes that transform every transcript the moment you stop recording. Tell SkipKey "express this formally for my boss" and a rambling voice note becomes a polished message. Set up "translate to German" and your spoken English arrives ready to send. Any instruction, any provider — SkipKey applies it hands-free on every recording.

Your voice never leaves your phone. One small purchase — it keeps working privately, forever.
