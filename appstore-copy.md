## Description (4000 chars max)

SkipKey turns your voice into text in any app on your iPhone — Messages, Notes, Mail, anywhere you can type.

Unlike other dictation tools, SkipKey never sends your voice to a server. Everything runs on-device using a bundled AI model, so it works privately, reliably, and even without an internet connection.

**Fully local. Fully private.**

Your voice never leaves your phone. No cloud processing, no account, no subscription. What you say stays between you and your iPhone — always.

**Works everywhere, automatically.**

SkipKey is a real iOS keyboard. Switch to it in any app, tap Start Recording, speak naturally, and your words appear the moment you stop. No copy-pasting, no switching apps, no waiting.

**25 languages. Zero setup.**

Speak in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, and 16 more European languages. SkipKey detects your language from your voice — no selector, no configuration required.

**Your workflow. Your rules.**

This is where SkipKey goes beyond ordinary dictation. Create custom processing modes that transform every transcript automatically the moment you stop recording.

- Working in a professional context? Set up a "Semi-formal" mode that quietly cleans up filler words and tightens your phrasing before it hits the text field.
- Messaging a friend or family member abroad? Create a "German" or "French" mode and your spoken English arrives already translated.
- Taking quick notes? A "Clean up" mode removes the ums and restores punctuation without changing a word of meaning.

Name the mode, write a one-line instruction, and the button appears in the keyboard. Switch between modes with a single tap between recordings.

**Track what you save.**

SkipKey counts every word you dictate and estimates how much typing time you've saved — today, this week, and all time. Most users are surprised how fast it adds up.

**One purchase. No subscription.**

SkipKey is a small one-time purchase. No monthly fee, no upsell, no account. Buy it once and it keeps working — privately, locally, forever.
